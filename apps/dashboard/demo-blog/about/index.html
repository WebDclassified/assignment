About this journal

Fieldnotes is written by no one in particular, about anything that rewards a second look. It exists here for a practical reason: to give the Pathlens tracker a quiet, text-heavy site to observe — long pages, real links, and clicks scattered the way readers actually scatter them.

Every visit creates an anonymous session, every page load records one page view, and every click is stored with its position. Nothing you type is ever collected, because there is nothing to type.
